Touch Grass is an open-weight AI outdoor companion that turns 15–60 minutes of free time into a tiny real-world adventure.

Tell it how much time you have and start from where you are. Gemma creates a personalized micro-quest. Once the quest starts, the app gets out of the way: the plan is cached offline, the screen becomes minimal, and optional on-device Gemma vision can turn things you discover into the next challenge.

The AI doesn't want your attention. It wants you to stop using your phone.
